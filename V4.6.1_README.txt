BABY INVESTOR V4.6.1 — ANALYZER INTEGRATION HOTFIX

Fixes the live V4.6 crash at analyzer stage 15/16.

Root cause:
V4.6 analyzer passed accounting_quality and valuation into the legacy
StockScoringEngine.score() method, but that method intentionally accepts only
market, technical, fundamentals, risk, and primary_financial. Accounting quality
and valuation belong to the new sovereign DecisionEngine, which consumes the
completed analyzer report after StockAnalyzer finishes.

Fix:
- Removed the two invalid legacy scoring kwargs from analyzer.py.
- Did NOT add fake/unused parameters to StockScoringEngine.
- Accounting quality and valuation remain in the analyzer report and are consumed
  by DecisionEngine with V4.6 weights.
- Cleaned the synthetic RSI divide-by-zero warning.
- No financial, accounting, valuation, risk, macro, market, event, decision, or
  trade-plan formulas changed.

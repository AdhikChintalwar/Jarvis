BABY INVESTOR V4.4 — Final Investment Committee Governance

Architecture
------------
Deterministic engines
    -> EvidenceBuilder
    -> EvidenceRegistry
    -> FinalCommitteeGuard
    -> Nemotron synthesis
    -> ClaimValidator / ReportValidator
    -> deterministic governance
    -> final research ranking

Core rule
---------
Nemotron is the investment committee, not the financial-data source and not the
risk authority.

V4.4 adds
---------
- deterministic committee baseline from:
  financial health, accounting quality, valuation, event intelligence,
  macro regime, advanced market intelligence, and inverse unified risk
- confidence x coverage authority weighting
- missing modules omitted rather than scored bearish
- deterministic maximum-decision ceilings
- hard-risk overrides cannot be talked away by Nemotron
- VERY_HIGH risk => maximum WATCH
- EXTREME risk => maximum WAIT
- weak committee evidence => maximum WATCH
- final score = deterministic baseline plus integrity-weighted minority LLM input
- final confidence capped by evidence authority and claim integrity
- final ranking sorted by governed score, not the LLM's proposed rank
- provenance/reliability mappings for all V3.7-V4.2 evidence layers
- audit fields showing deterministic score, LLM score, authority, coverage,
  decision ceiling, constraints, and module contributions

Important
---------
committee_score is a research-attractiveness synthesis, not a predicted return.
V4.4 does not place orders and does not authorize real-money execution.
Yahoo/yfinance evidence remains prototype/secondary where applicable.

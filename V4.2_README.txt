BABY INVESTOR V4.2 — Unified Risk Model

Purpose
-------
Consolidates verified risk evidence into one deterministic safety layer while
keeping investment quality and risk conceptually separate.

Dimensions
----------
- financial
- accounting
- valuation
- market / realized volatility
- liquidity
- SEC / event
- macro + market environment

Core safety semantics
---------------------
- Missing evidence = UNKNOWN, not bearish.
- Dimension scores use 0 low risk -> 100 extreme risk.
- Evidence confidence affects aggregation authority.
- Extreme 20D annualized realized volatility (>=90%) creates a hard risk floor.
- Severe low dollar-volume liquidity (<$1M average/day) creates a hard risk floor.
- Registration / mention-only SEC events do NOT trigger dilution/event overrides.
- Valuation risk is bounded because expensive does not equal unsafe.
- position_risk_multiplier is only a future sizing safety input.
- V4.2 still does not submit or authorize orders.
- Existing StockScore is intentionally unchanged.

Real-test expectation
---------------------
CRWD should be a useful hard-override validation case if its realized volatility
remains around the ~98% observed in V4.1.

Known limitation
----------------
Liquidity coverage depends on whether the current analyzer's market-data/info
object exposes average dollar volume or enough price/volume fields. If not,
liquidity must remain UNKNOWN rather than being guessed.

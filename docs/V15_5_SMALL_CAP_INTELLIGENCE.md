New deterministic layers: flow strength, 5-session flow persistence, up/down-volume ratio, weighted close-location value, 20-session price retention, ATR volatility, market phase, catalyst source/event classification, capital-structure risk, setup type, monitoring signal, pipeline telemetry, point-in-time replay.

Every research email separates OBSERVED FACTS from BABY INTERPRETATION and includes contradicting evidence and next conditions.

Quantity authority remains USER_SELECTED. AI execution authority NONE. Real-money execution DISABLED.

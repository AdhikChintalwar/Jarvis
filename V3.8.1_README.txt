BABY INVESTOR V3.8.1 — Valuation Semantics & Calibration

- Explicit PROFITABLE / LOSS_MAKING / UNKNOWN states.
- Explicit FCF_POSITIVE / FCF_NEGATIVE / UNKNOWN states.
- Negative/zero earnings => trailing P/E and earnings yield are N.M. (None).
- Negative/zero FCF => P/FCF, EV/FCF and FCF yield are N.M.
- Separate absolute valuation score and growth-adjusted score.
- Growth adjustment is only a 20% modifier; it cannot excuse extreme absolute valuation.
- Market-data date and verified-financial period provenance are exposed separately.
- Missing evidence remains neutral.

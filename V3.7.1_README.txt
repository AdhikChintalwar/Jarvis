BABY INVESTOR V3.7.1 — Accounting Semantics & Score Calibration

Changes:
- OCF/net-income cash conversion is calculated only for positive net income.
- Loss-making companies use a separate cash-generation-despite-losses signal.
- Positive accounting signals use diminishing returns instead of linear saturation.
- Extreme positive scores have evidence-depth ceilings.
- Historical trend authority scales with annual-history depth.
- AccountingQualityReport exposes history_periods and history_confidence.
- Missing evidence remains neutral and lowers evidence depth/confidence only.

BABY INVESTOR V5.5 — HISTORICAL RESEARCH & VALIDATION PLATFORM

Built on frozen V5.0 point-in-time contracts.

Added:
- Official SEC submissions-history adapter including historical submissions files.
- EDGAR complete-submission acceptance timestamp extraction.
- Company Facts filtered by accession numbers actually available at simulated time.
- SEC accession/form/concept provenance on historical facts.
- Deterministic historical financial resolver and FCF.
- Historical market slicing.
- Walk-forward/out-of-sample fold generator.
- Equal-weight and simple momentum benchmark framework.
- Validation audit reporting model version, lookahead, survivorship, PIT status.
- HistoricalResearchPlatform orchestration.

Important limitations intentionally preserved:
- This version does NOT claim a complete historical listed-equity/delisting database.
  PointInTimeUniverse still requires a dated membership ledger.
- Company Facts is filtered by accession to prevent later filings/restatements from
  leaking backward, but full filing-instance reconstruction remains preferable for
  difficult XBRL taxonomy/dimension cases.
- Historical financial resolver is conservative and does not yet replay every V4.x
  financial/accounting/valuation formula.
- No real-money execution.
- AI scoring authority remains 0%; execution authority remains 0%.

SEC_USER_AGENT must be configured in .env.

BABY INVESTOR V3.8 — Valuation Intelligence

Adds deterministic valuation based on current market cap plus verified SEC-primary denominators:
- trailing P/E
- price/sales
- enterprise value
- EV/sales
- price/FCF
- EV/FCF
- earnings yield
- FCF yield
- trailing P/E / verified revenue-growth diagnostic
- secondary/prototype forward P/E kept explicitly low-authority
- valuation evidence confidence, coverage, positives, red flags and unknowns
- StockScoringEngine and committee-evidence integration

Important:
This version does NOT claim intrinsic/fair value. Multiple bands are broad deterministic heuristics.
Peer-relative valuation and historical valuation ranges require a later trusted historical/peer market-data layer.

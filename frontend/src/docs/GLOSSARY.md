# Glossary

## AI
Artificial Intelligence. In Baby, AI may help explain information, but AI scoring authority is 0% and AI execution authority is NONE.

## Alpaca
The broker/data service used for the PAPER-broker integration and production execution-grade market-data path.

## API
Application Programming Interface. A structured way for software systems to exchange information.

## Ask
The lowest price currently offered by a seller.

## Bid
The highest price currently offered by a buyer.

## Candidate
A stock that passed a discovery/scanner step and was selected for deeper research. Candidate does not mean approved trade.

## Catalyst
An event that may be relevant to a company or its stock. Baby does not automatically claim that a catalyst caused a price move.

## Confidence
A Baby field describing confidence/coverage characteristics of the deterministic research output. It is not a guarantee of return.

## Coverage
How much of the expected evidence/checks is available and resolved.

## CANDIDATE
A Baby research-decision label indicating the symbol remains a research candidate under the current model.

## BLOCKED
A required production gate did not pass. The proposal is not currently eligible.

## DATA_ISSUE
A monitoring state indicating that missing, stale or invalid data prevents normal interpretation.

## EMA
Exponential Moving Average. A moving average that gives more weight to recent prices.

## Eligible
The deterministic PAPER proposal passed the required current gates and is ready for human review/confirmation.

## Entry
The planned price/condition where a trade setup would become actionable under the deterministic plan.

## Entry Triggered
A deterministic flag indicating that the current setup condition has been activated.

## Evidence
A sourced metric, calculation, event or rule output used by Baby's research process.

## Execution Authority
Permission to submit a broker action. Baby's AI execution authority is NONE.

## Execution-Grade Quote
The current quote source Baby accepts for production proposal gating. It must meet freshness/quality requirements.

## FCF
Free Cash Flow. Cash generated by the business after capital spending, according to the source definition used by the pipeline.

## FOMC
Federal Open Market Committee. The Federal Reserve group responsible for major U.S. monetary-policy decisions.

## Hard Risk Override
A deterministic risk condition strong enough to constrain/block a candidate regardless of other attractive evidence.

## IEX
Investors Exchange. The current Alpaca market-data feed used in Baby's production execution-quote path.

## Invalidation
The price/condition where the original trade thesis/setup is considered invalid under the plan.

## LLM
Large Language Model. A type of AI model. In Baby, an LLM does not control deterministic scoring, risk override or execution.

## Monitored Setup
A research/trade setup Baby is watching over time. It is not a broker position and not an order.

## NOT_ESTABLISHED
Baby's label when causality has not been proven, such as whether a news headline caused a price move.

## OHLCV
Open, High, Low, Close, Volume. Standard price-bar data.

## PAPER
Simulated brokerage environment using non-real funds.

## Paper Order
A simulated order submitted to the Alpaca PAPER environment.

## Paper Position
A simulated holding that exists in the Alpaca PAPER brokerage account.

## Portfolio Gate
A deterministic check of account/portfolio constraints before a PAPER proposal can become eligible.

## Production Decision
The final deterministic gate result after research, trade setup, risk, quote and portfolio checks.

## Pullback
A setup that waits for price to move back toward a planned zone instead of entering immediately.

## Breakout
A setup that waits for price to move through a defined trigger/level with the required confirmation.

## R/R
Risk/Reward ratio. Compares planned downside to a target with the plan's expected upside to that target.

## Research Attractiveness
Baby's deterministic score describing how attractive the research evidence looks under its model. It is not a return forecast.

## REVIEW
A state indicating that available evidence requires attention rather than a clean pass.

## RSI
Relative Strength Index. A momentum indicator commonly expressed from 0 to 100.

## Scanner
The discovery process that searches for market candidates.

## SETUP_READY
A monitored/setup state indicating that deterministic conditions are ready for review. It does not mean an order has been placed.

## SMA
Simple Moving Average. Average price over a specified number of periods.

## Spread
Difference between the bid and ask price. Wide spreads can make execution quality worse.

## Target 1 / T1
The first planned profit-taking/reference target in the deterministic trade plan.

## Target 2 / T2
The second planned target in the deterministic trade plan.

## Thesis
The structured body of evidence supporting and challenging the research case.

## UNKNOWN
Baby does not have enough reliable evidence to resolve the field/check. UNKNOWN is not automatically positive or negative.

## Unified Risk
Baby's deterministic layer that combines risk evidence/constraints into a single risk assessment.

## V11
Baby's deterministic research-attractiveness / thesis decision layer used by the current research architecture.

## V12
Baby's production decision/gating layer used after research to determine whether a PAPER proposal passes required conditions.

## WAITING
The plan exists, but the current entry/setup requirements are not active.

## Webhook
An automatic message sent from one system to another when an event occurs.

BABY INVESTOR V3.9 — Catalyst, News & SEC Event Intelligence

Adds:
- Unified deterministic event records with event date, source, evidence class,
  direction, materiality, confidence, age and freshness decay.
- SEC filing metadata is primary evidence.
- News headline classification is explicitly secondary evidence.
- S-1/S-3/F-1/F-3/424B forms never automatically mean completed dilution.
- Form 4 never automatically means insider buying or selling.
- 8-K/6-K never automatically receive bullish/bearish direction from form alone.
- Deep SEC going-concern evidence can create a primary negative event.
- Financing language is preserved as risk evidence but not proof of issuance.
- Old events decay out of the active catalyst set.
- Committee evidence now receives event intelligence.
- Existing CatalystEngine remains for backward compatibility.

V3.9 intentionally does not yet parse Form 4 transaction tables or 8-K item text
into a full corporate-event ontology. Those require dedicated primary parsers.

BABY INVESTOR V5.0 — POINT-IN-TIME RESEARCH & BACKTESTING FOUNDATION

New architecture
investor/point_in_time/
  models.py          immutable evidence/snapshot/backtest contracts
  availability.py    no-lookahead availability policy
  snapshot_store.py  SQLite historical research snapshots
  universe.py        dated universe membership / survivorship guard
  engine.py          point-in-time research snapshot builder
  backtest.py        next-bar execution research backtester

Core invariants
- Fundamental evidence requires available_at <= simulated decision time.
- Period end alone NEVER makes a filing historically available.
- Future evidence is blocked and audited.
- Point-in-time universe membership is required.
- Signals generated on bar t execute on the next available bar.
- Transaction costs reduce cash/equity.
- Backtest reports requested vs actual dates.
- V5.0 does NOT pretend today's SEC Company Facts endpoint is a historical archive.
- Real historical filings must be reconstructed from filing timestamps before Baby's
  deterministic DecisionEngine can be honestly replayed point-in-time.
- AI scoring authority remains 0%.
- AI execution authority remains 0%.

This is the infrastructure layer. V5.1 should build SEC filing-time reconstruction and
historical market/universe adapters on top of these contracts.

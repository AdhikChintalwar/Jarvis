BABY INVESTOR V4.2.1 — Unified Risk Stabilization

Fixes from V4.2
---------------
1. Financial evidence authority
   - Reads V3.6.2 evidence_confidence / evidence_coverage.
   - Keeps compatibility fallbacks for generic confidence/coverage names.
   - Prevents financial risk from being calculated with zero authority.

2. Deterministic liquidity
   - StockAnalyzer calculates 20-session average dollar volume directly from
     the already-loaded Close × Volume history.
   - No additional API request is required.
   - Liquidity evidence includes ADV20, average share volume, sessions, as-of,
     source and authority.
   - Relative volume remains a participation signal, NOT a liquidity proxy.

3. Missing-neutral semantics remain unchanged
   - If history cannot support ADV20, liquidity remains UNKNOWN.
   - Missing liquidity does not create a bearish/risk penalty.

4. Hard overrides remain unchanged
   - >=90% 20D annualized realized volatility imposes an 80 risk-score floor.
   - Severe ADV20 below $1M/day can impose a liquidity hard override.

5. Safety boundary
   - Unified risk remains deterministic.
   - position_risk_multiplier is a future sizing safety input only.
   - No real-money order is placed or authorized.
   - Existing StockScore remains unchanged.
